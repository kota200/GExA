What is GExA?

Grass Expression Atlas (GExA) is an interactive web-based resource for rapid exploration of gene expression across diverse tissues, developmental stages, and conditions in grass species.

GExA provides standardized RNA-seq expression profiles and enables users to visualize expression patterns of genes of interest through an interactive web interface.

Covered species

Pearl millet, foxtail millet, proso millet, finger millet, barley,sorghum, and rice

Main functions

Interactive visualization of gene expression patterns across tissues, developmental stages, treatments, and cultivars

Data source

Publicly available RNA-seq datasets processed and curated metadata

Update policy

GExA is planned to be updated twice a year. Major updates may include the addition of newly available public RNA-seq datasets, expansion to additional grass species, refinement of manually curated metadata, and correction of reported errors.

Minor bug fixes and corrections may be released as needed.

Last updated: 25 August 2026 — Updated the foxtail millet reference genome to the Yugu1 (T2T) assembly.
